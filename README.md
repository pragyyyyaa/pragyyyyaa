## 🚀 `Interests & Work`
My work reflects a blend of curiosity and practical application. I have explored **demand forecasting** using TES & ARMA to model consumer trends in whisky, craft beer, and white wine, capturing seasonality for campaign and inventory planning. I also worked on **loan status classification** through logistic regression and decision trees, where SMOTE was applied to address imbalance and uncover critical borrower risk factors. In the area of business strategy, I pursued **sales optimization and EDA**, identifying both revenue and fulfillment gaps, while also developing a **Power BI sales dashboard** for KPI tracking and reporting. Beyond analytics, I initiated **MealBridge**, a non-profit platform connecting donors and volunteers to support underprivileged communities — a project that combines empathy with innovation. These pursuits not only highlight my interests but also reflect my growing capabilities as a data-driven problem solver.
